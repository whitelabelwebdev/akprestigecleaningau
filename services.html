What we offer

Bond Cleaning

Our Bond Cleaning service is designed to prepare your property for its final inspection and help leave it in excellent condition. We complete a comprehensive clean throughout the home, paying close attention to the areas commonly inspected by property managers.

This service includes:

Kitchen8 tasks
  • Cupboards, drawers and shelves cleaned inside and out
  • Cupboard tops, doors and benchtops wiped down
  • Splashbacks cleaned
  • Oven cleaned inside and out, including racks and trays*
  • Stovetop, elements, rangehood and filters degreased and cleaned
  • Exhaust fan cleaned
  • Interior windows, tracks and sills cleaned
  • Light fittings dusted and wiped
Bathrooms & Powder Rooms8 tasks
  • Showers, shower screens and fittings scrubbed and cleaned
  • Baths cleaned and polished*
  • Toilets thoroughly cleaned and sanitised
  • Basins and vanities cleaned
  • Mirrors polished
  • Door frames, light switches and exhaust fans cleaned
  • Interior windows, tracks and sills cleaned
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
Bedrooms5 tasks
  • Wardrobes, cupboards, drawers and shelves cleaned
  • Mirrors cleaned
  • Door frames, power points and light switches wiped
  • Ceiling fans and air-conditioning vents dusted
  • Interior windows, tracks and sills cleaned
Laundry6 tasks
  • Cupboards and surfaces wiped down
  • Laundry tub cleaned
  • Exhaust fans and light fittings cleaned
  • Door frames, walls, skirting boards and light switches spot cleaned
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Interior windows, tracks and sills cleaned
Living, Dining & Common Areas9 tasks
  • All surfaces dusted and wiped
  • Light fittings cleaned
  • Cobwebs removed
  • Ceiling fans and air-conditioning units dusted
  • Power points and light switches cleaned
  • Interior windows, tracks and sills cleaned
  • Carpeted areas vacuumed
  • Timber, tile and vinyl floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Walls and doors spot cleaned where required

Please note:

  • 1Wall washing can be completed at an additional cost if required.
  • 2If an oven requires excessive degreasing or restoration beyond a standard bond clean, an additional charge may apply.
  • 3While every effort is made to remove mould, calcium build-up and staining, complete removal cannot always be guaranteed where permanent damage or deterioration has occurred.
