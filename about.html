About the Owners

Hi, we’re Bailey and Jacob, the owners of AK Prestige Cleaning.

We started AK Prestige Cleaning with a clear goal: to provide reliable, high-quality residential cleaning services that make life easier for busy households across the Gold Coast.

We understand how much of a difference a clean, well-maintained home can make. That’s why we’re committed to providing a professional, detail-focused service that our clients can feel completely confident welcoming into their homes.

We work closely with our team to ensure every clean is completed with care, consistency and attention to detail, maintaining the high standard that AK Prestige Cleaning is known for.

As a growing local business, we’re proud to offer a personalised approach to cleaning. Whether you need regular weekly or fortnightly cleaning, a one-off clean, a bond clean or additional services tailored to your home, our goal is to make the entire experience as easy and reliable as possible.

For us, AK Prestige Cleaning is about more than simply having a clean home. Our aim is to give you back your time while leaving your home feeling fresh, comfortable and beautifully cared for.
